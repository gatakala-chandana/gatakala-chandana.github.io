How this metric works

{{ md.title }}

{{ md.goal }}

Customer Activity is 75% of your Location Performance Score (LPS), a weekly score out of 100% that resets every Monday. It measures how many of your location's customers had at least one qualifying activity this week. The other 25% comes from completing a daily checklist on each production day.

How your LPS is built

Customer Activity 75%
Checklists 25%

Example: you touched all 20 customers but missed one of five checklist days.

PartScoreWeightPoints Customer Activity100%× 0.7575 Daily Checklists80%× 0.2520 LPS95%

What counts

  • Entering a new Quality Alert or Near Miss.
  • Logging a Proactive Audit.
  • Entering an Action Taken on an existing Quality Alert or Near Miss, including ones from another plant.

Each customer counts once, no matter how many activities you log for them.

What doesn't count

  • Marking another plant's alert “Checking for Issue” or “No Issue Found.” An Action Taken on a found issue does count.

Which customers count

Customers with an active agreement at your location this week, including agreements that start or end mid-week.

As-Needed, 0-Unit, Level 0, and shut-down agreements are excluded from both the target and your total, so they can't help or hurt you.

Why your number might look off

Full-week shutdown
You get no score (not a zero), and the week is left out of your averages.
Partial shutdown
You're scored normally.
Coverage
Activity from any rep at your location counts, including a temp.

Your Effectiveness Ratio compares the discrepant material you catch in Near Misses to what escalates into Quality Alerts. A higher ratio means more issues are being caught and contained before they become official OEM non-conformances.

How it's calculated

Total discrepant material in Near Misses divided by total discrepant material in Quality Alerts, always shown against 1.

60 parts
÷
Quality Alert material
20 parts

Ratio · 60 ÷ 20
3.0 : 1

What material counts

All material dispositioned as:

ScrappedHeldReturnedReworkedInstalled in Vehicle

Material on a draft doesn't count until the issue is submitted.

Why your number might look off

Timing
Material counts on the day it was added, not the day the issue was written.
Other plants' issues
Material counts at the location of whoever enters it. If you add material to another plant's Quality Alert, it counts for you. If they add material to yours, it counts for them.
Upgraded Near Misses
Material entered while an issue was a Near Miss stays on the Near Miss side. Only material added after the upgrade counts as Quality Alert.
No Near Miss material
Shows as 0.0 : 1.
No Quality Alert material
Still shows a ratio.

Time On-Site is how long you were at your assigned service locations each day. It's measured automatically from your check-ins and check-outs, so you don't need to record anything.

How it's calculated

Your daily total is the time spent on site across all your assigned locations, with overlapping time counted only once.

Location A · 4 hrs
Location B · 2 hrs
10am11am12pm1pm2pm
Counted 4 hrs

Checked into one location 10:00am–2:00pm and another 12:00pm–2:00pm is 4 hours, not 6.

Which locations count

  • Your primary service locations.
  • The sequencers, feeders, integrators, and Tier 1s attached to them.
  • Any location you're temporarily assigned to.

Your goal

Your goal is set for you and may differ from other reps'. It's based on your daily goal hours and your normal workdays, and your weekly goal is the sum of your daily goals.

Your goal is zero on these days, and zero-goal days are left out of your averages:

Non-workdaysPTO or leaveAll primary locations shut downWeekends, unless normal workdays for you

Why your number might look off

Midnight split
Time is split at midnight. On site from 10pm Tuesday to 4am Wednesday shows as 2 hours Tuesday and 4 hours Wednesday.
Still checked in
Today's total keeps counting up until you check out.
Keep location permissions on
For the most accurate time, make sure the app has location permissions enabled and stays running in the background. If you forget to check out, the day closes automatically.
