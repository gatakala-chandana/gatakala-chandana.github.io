    Arizona Water Chatbot

    Your trusted chatbot companion for all things related to water in the Grand Canyon State. Let’s dive in together and make a difference for Arizona’s water future!

    FAQs

    What topics does the Arizona Water Chatbot cover?

    Blue answers questions about Arizona’s water: drought, the Colorado River, Lake Mead and Lake Powell, groundwater, where our water comes from, water quality, water policy, and ways to conserve water at home.

    How can I personalize my experience with the Chatbot?

    Pick Short, Bullet points or Descriptive answers from the menu in the message bar, or change any answer’s style afterwards with Edit Response Type. Blue also greets you by the name you entered when you signed in.

    What kind of updates does the Arizona Water Chatbot receive?

    This is a free research preview. Answers are written fresh for each question, and the features change based on your feedback, so use Helpful on answers you like. Always check important facts with the agencies listed under Sources.
